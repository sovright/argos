Argos

Argos is local-first, self-custody recovery software for people who already control legacy ZecWallet Lite seed phrases. Argos helps validate a seed phrase locally, derive legacy Zcash wallet addresses, scan wallet history through user-selected lightwalletd endpoints, prepare sweep transactions, and broadcast user-approved transactions to the Zcash network.

Argos is software only. Argos is not a bank, exchange, broker, dealer, custodian, fiduciary, money transmitter, payment processor, financial institution, investment adviser, tax adviser, legal adviser, or asset recovery service.
